Government & Public Buildings in Almere

Almere has several important public buildings that serve the community. These buildings play an important role in the city's daily life, providing services, support, and shared spaces for residents and visitors alike.

Public buildings at the heart of Almere

From the city hall to libraries and public facilities, Almere's public buildings are designed to support daily life, foster community connection, and make essential services accessible across the city.

Stadhuis Almere - the city hall

The city hall is a prominent civic landmark that serves as the administrative heart of Almere. It provides a visible point of contact for residents, visitors, and municipal services, helping shape the city's daily operations and community life.

The building's public-facing design reflects the city's commitment to openness, accessibility, and modern civic identity, making it a natural starting point for anyone exploring Almere's public institutions.

More Public buildings in Almere

Almere Council Chamber

Located inside City Hall, this modern chamber features a striking circular layout, a grand central skylight, and the official city coat of arms displayed on a curved marble wall. It is also used for official council meetings and civic ceremonies.

Almere City Centre (Citymall)

A vibrant public square in downtown Almere, showcasing unique modern architecture layered with residential spaces above retail stores. The central stairs display "ALLY"—the popular local nickname for the city.

De Nieuwe Biblotheek

Almere's central library is a place to read, learn and explore ideas. Its terraced interior brings books and shared spaces together, while the building also incorporates shops, offices and apartments in the city centre.

Almere Centrum Station

Almere Centrum station is a vital transport hub that connects residents, commuters, and visitors with the wider region. It plays a key role in the city’s mobility network, supporting access to public services, shopping, culture, and daily travel.
