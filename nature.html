Water & Nature in Almere

Water is an important part of Almere. The Weerwater and surrounding green areas offer beautiful views and places to relax. Most people enjoy walking, cycling and spending time outdoors.The Weerwater and canals provides beautiful scenery.

Explore the outdoors

Almere has beautiful lakes, parks and green spaces where people can walk, cycle and enjoy nature. Here are some of the best places to visit.

Cherry Blossom Street

A street lined with 850 Japanese cherry trees forming a vibrant pink tunnel every spring. A major local landmark for photography and the annual Cherry Blossom Festival.

Walking & Cycling Routes

Almere has many walking and cycling Routes through parks, forest and along the water. These routes are a great way to explore the city and enjoy the natural surroundings.

Lakes & Waterways

Almere is built around several lakes and canals. The waterways give the city a unique character and offer beautiful views, especially on quiet days.

Green Spaces

Almere has many open green spaces where people go on picnics, camping and enjoy fresh air and nature.
